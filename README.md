# 🧭 Prompt Navigator

**Stop scrolling. Jump straight to any message you've ever sent.**

Prompt Navigator is a free browser extension for people who use AI chatbots every day. It adds a clean side panel that lists every message you've typed in a conversation — so you can find, re-read, copy, save, and jump to any of them instantly.

No account. No login. Nothing uploaded anywhere. It all runs inside your browser.

---

## The problem it solves

Long AI conversations are a mess to navigate. You typed something great 40 messages ago and now you have to scroll forever to find it. You write the same prompt three times because you forgot how you phrased it last week. You want to copy your best questions but there's no easy way to do it.

Prompt Navigator fixes all of that.

---

## What you get

### Jump to any message instantly
Click any prompt in the panel and the page scrolls straight to it — highlighted with a brief flash so you know exactly where it is. No more manual scrolling.

### Search everything you've said
Type a word and the list filters in real time. Every match is highlighted. Find any prompt in any conversation in under a second.

### Save your best prompts as favourites
Star any prompt to save it. Your favourites stay saved even after you close the browser. Open the ⭐ tab anytime to find them — or export them for later use.

### See what you've been working on
The Recent tab shows the last 10 prompts you navigated to, with the platform name and how long ago you sent them. Great for picking up where you left off.

### Read the full text on hover
Prompts are shown in a compact list, but hover over any one and the full text pops up in a tooltip. You'll never have to wonder what a truncated prompt says.

### Copy in one click
Every prompt has a copy button. No selecting text, no right-clicking — one tap and it's on your clipboard.

### Export your prompts
Download your entire conversation's prompts as a **JSON**, **CSV**, or **PDF** file. Keep a record of your best thinking, share it with your team, or drop it into a document.

### Floating panel (optional)
Enable a floating 🧭 button that lives on the AI page itself. Click it to open the navigator without leaving the chat.

### Dark & light theme
Follows your system preference by default. Switch manually in Settings if you prefer.

## Install

### Chrome, Edge, Brave, or any Chromium browser

> The Chrome Web Store listing is coming soon. Until then, you can install it manually — it takes about 2 minutes and no technical knowledge is needed.

**Step 1 — Download**
Go to the [Releases page](https://github.com/withrvr/prompt-navigator/releases) and download the latest `prompt-navigator-chrome-vX.Y.Z.zip` file.

**Step 2 — Unzip it**
Extract the zip to any folder on your computer (e.g. your Downloads folder). You'll get a folder called something like `prompt-navigator-chrome-v0.2.0`.

**Step 3 — Open your browser's extensions page**
- **Chrome:** type `chrome://extensions` in the address bar and press Enter
- **Edge:** type `edge://extensions`
- **Brave:** type `brave://extensions`

**Step 4 — Enable Developer Mode**
In the top-right corner of the extensions page, toggle on **Developer mode**.

**Step 5 — Load the extension**
Click **Load unpacked** and select the folder you unzipped in Step 2.

That's it. The 🧭 icon will appear in your toolbar. Pin it for quick access.

> **Updates:** When a new release is available, repeat these steps and point "Load unpacked" at the new folder. Chrome will replace the old version automatically.

### Firefox

Download `prompt-navigator-firefox-vX.Y.Z.zip` from the [Releases page](https://github.com/withrvr/prompt-navigator/releases).

Open **about:addons** → click the gear icon → **Install Add-on From File…** → select the zip.

## How to use

1. Go to any supported AI chat and open a conversation.
2. Click the **🧭** icon in your toolbar.
3. Every message you've sent appears as a numbered list.
4. **Click any item** to jump straight to it in the chat.
5. Use the **search bar** at the top to filter by keyword.
6. **Hover** any item to read the full text.
7. Click **⭐** to save a prompt to your favourites.
8. Click **📋** to copy a prompt to your clipboard.
9. Click **📤 Export** to download your prompts as JSON, CSV, or PDF.
10. Click **⚙️** to change your theme or turn on the floating panel.

## Privacy

Prompt Navigator does **not** collect, transmit, or store any of your data.

- Everything runs locally inside your browser.
- No servers. No analytics. No accounts.
- Your prompts never leave your device.
- The only storage used is your browser's built-in extension storage — for your saved favourites, theme preference, and settings. This stays on your machine.

You can verify this yourself — the full source code is on GitHub.
